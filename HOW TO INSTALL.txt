WHAT CHANGED (your notes of 26 November)
  1. No deal lists. "I found 39 Black Friday tech deals actually worth shopping", the other
     deal posts ("... drops to $60 for Black Friday") and buying guides ("Best Power Banks
     (2026): My Picks After Testing Over 100") no longer show. News ABOUT Black Friday stays:
     "Rising interest rates fail to dampen Black Friday sales frenzy", "Four in five Black
     Friday deals are duds, research finds" (2.4 hid those two; 2.5 shows them).
  2. Only articles. "Membership" (the warontherocks.com/membership-2/ link), subscribe, donate,
     newsletter, login, shop and account pages, and archive, topic and stock-quote pages are
     left out.
  3. Every headline has words. "- The New York Times" and other items that are only the
     publisher's name, or one or two words, are left out. The feeds carry no real headline for
     them (checked on 650 such items), so there is nothing to recover.
  4. A calmer pace: a build every 15 minutes (2.4: every 10).
  Measured on exactly the same recorded news (26 Nov): the 2.4 page showed 30 such items (20 deal
  posts, 5 non-article pages, 5 without a headline - your three examples among them); the 2.5
  page shows 0. Every removal was read by eye so no real news is lost, and the build log lists
  every item left out, so a wrong removal is easy to spot.
  Untouched: nuzu-bundle.js, sw.js, manifest.json, .well-known, twa-manifest.json and every
  Play Store file.

HOW TO UPLOAD
  1. Open https://github.com/NUZUNEWS/nuzunews.github.io
  2. Add file > Upload files. Drag in bot.py, version.json, CHANGELOG.md and README.md from this
     folder. Commit message, for example: "NUZU 2.5". Commit changes.
  3. Still on GitHub, open the folder .github, then workflows. Add file > Upload files. Drag in
     refresh.yml from this folder's .github\workflows folder (it replaces the old one).
     Commit changes.
  4. Actions tab > refresh > Run workflow (this starts the new loop at once).

CHECK (about 20 minutes after step 4)
  - https://nuzunews.github.io/health.json shows "version": "2.5.0".
  - The Science, Tech & Health section shows no "... Black Friday deals" lists.
  - Actions tab: the refresh run shows "Build 2" about 15 minutes after "Build 1".

YOUR QUESTION: IS THE PACE OK? (measured on the live site, 27 Nov)
  Every 10 minutes worked: 98 site builds in the last 20 hours, all successful; 0 feeds failed;
  no "too many requests" (429) answers. But it ran close to the edges. GitHub Pages allows about
  10 builds an hour (2.4 made 6-7, plus the old daily-content.yml at 00:05 UTC). Each build makes
  about 500 requests (327 of them to Google News) from GitHub's shared addresses, which Google can
  slow down or block when many bots use them; one site (Harvard Business Review) did answer 429
  to a test build from this PC today. Every 15 minutes means 4 builds an hour and a third fewer
  requests (about 2,000 an hour instead of 3,000), and the news is at most about 15 minutes old.
  Nothing shows a ban. Conditional requests ("only send it if it changed") were measured and not
  added: only 4 of 25 feeds support them, Google News not at all.
